Structural Repair & Waterproofing Services

Engineered structural defense systems designed to permanently stop water intrusion, repair foundation cracks, and lift settling footings.

Foundation Crack Repair

Structural & Moisture Barrier
The Problem:

Hydrostatic water pressure forces moisture through poured concrete fissures or block wall mortar joints, threatening basement dry integrity.

Our Solution:

Dual-stage structural epoxy injection to fuse concrete fractures combined with high-tensile carbon fiber grid straps to prevent future wall bowing.

Key Benefits & Specs:
  • • Resists up to 10,000 PSI concrete bonding strength
  • • Prevents radon gas and damp soil air infiltration
  • • Fast 2 to 4 hour clean interior installation

Foundation Piering & Repair

Deep Structural Stabilization
The Problem:

Unstable subfloor clay soils cause foundation footings to drop, creating diagonal drywall cracks, sticking doors, and floor tilt.

Our Solution:

Galvanized steel helical piers and push piers hydraulically driven past weak soil strata into bedrock to stabilize and lift foundation footings.

Key Benefits & Specs:
  • • Restores structural load distribution
  • • Engineer-calculated hydraulic lifting system
  • • Lifetime transferable footing support

Basement Waterproofing

Perimeter Drainage Track
The Problem:

Subterranean water tables exert cove joint pressure, causing floor seam leaks and flooded finished basements.

Our Solution:

Sub-slab interior perimeter drain tile system embedded in washed aggregate, capturing ground water and routing it to a high-capacity sump pit.

Key Benefits & Specs:
  • • Clog-resistant perimeter channel design
  • • Relieves hydrostatic floor pressure permanently
  • • Compatible with future basement finishing

Sump Pump Installation

Dual Pump Station
The Problem:

Single primary sump pumps fail during power outages, leading to sudden basement flooding during heavy rainstorms.

Our Solution:

Cast-iron primary pump paired with an automatic battery-backup secondary pump and check valves in a sealed structural basin.

Key Benefits & Specs:
  • • Discharges up to 4,200 Gallons Per Hour
  • • Smart battery charger & audible high-water alarm
  • • Quiet, energy-efficient motor assembly

Basement Sealing & Encapsulation

Vapor Control
The Problem:

Unsealed porous masonry walls release ground humidity, creating damp odors, mold growth, and drywall rot.

Our Solution:

Heavy-duty 20-mil antimicrobial vapor barrier wall liners sealed with mechanical fast-pins and waterproof polyurethane joint caulk.

Key Benefits & Specs:
  • • 100% moisture vapor diffusion seal
  • • Clean bright white semi-gloss finish
  • • Reduces home heating/cooling energy loss

Exterior Drainage & Water Management

Yard Drainage
The Problem:

Roof downspouts discharging water against foundation walls create soil erosion and heavy hydro-static pressure.

Our Solution:

Burying downspout extension lines, pop-up emitters, exterior French drains, and surface re-grading to safely discharge runoff away from home foundation.

Key Benefits & Specs:
  • • Prevents pool formation against foundation
  • • Heavy PVC smooth bore pipe (non-clogging)
  • • Blends seamlessly with yard turf
