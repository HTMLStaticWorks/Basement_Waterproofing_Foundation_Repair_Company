TRANSFORMATION ASSURANCE

How We Guarantee Every Transformation

Every structural repair and waterproofing project undergoes rigorous quality protocols before final sign-off.

1. Deflection & Alignment Audit

Laser levels and wall sensors measure structural deflection down to 1/16th inch before and after installing carbon fiber or helical pier supports.

2. Hydro-Pressure Flow Test

Interior sub-slab drainage and sump pumps are stress-tested under simulated high water volume to verify 100% water management capacity.

3. Transferable Lifetime Warranty

Homeowners receive a written engineer warranty certificate that transfers to future home buyers, protecting long-term property value.
