FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common foundation repair and waterproofing questions.

Hairline vertical cracks are often caused by normal concrete shrinkage during curing. However, horizontal cracks, stair-step masonry cracks, or cracks wider than 1/4 inch indicate active hydrostatic pressure or soil movement and require immediate professional structural evaluation.

Basement leaks stem from hydrostatic water pressure building up in the soil around foundation walls. Water finds the path of least resistance through floor joints (cove joint), porous masonry blocks, window wells, or wall tie rods.

No. If water intrusion is isolated to a single wall crack or improper downspout drainage, localized crack injection or exterior regrading may solve the issue without requiring full interior perimeter drainage.

Severe rainstorms that trigger basement flooding are often accompanied by electrical power outages. A dual battery-backup sump pump automatically activates when main power fails, preventing catastrophic water damage.

Most residential foundation crack injections take 2 to 4 hours. Full interior perimeter drainage and sump pump installations are typically completed within 1 to 2 days by our certified installation crew.
