COST DRIVERS

6 Variables That Determine Project Cost

No two foundations are identical. Here is how structural scope is calculated.

1. Issue Severity

Isolated hairline vertical cracks require simple resin injection, whereas bowed walls need carbon fiber straps or steel I-beams.

2. Foundation Material

Poured solid concrete walls differ from hollow cinder block or fieldstone masonry in terms of drilling and sealing labor.

3. Linear Footage

Full interior French drain installation pricing scales directly with the perimeter footage of your basement sub-slab.

4. Water Table Volume

High subterranean water volume requires dual 1/2 HP primary pumps and heavy-duty battery backup power systems.

5. Jobsite Access

Unfinished open basements cost less to work in than finished basements requiring drywall cutouts and stud protection.

6. Material Ratings

Industrial 20-mil wall vapor liners and heavy cast-iron pumps provide longer warranties than light consumer-grade products.
