COMPANY PROFILE

Protecting What Holds Your Home Together.

Built on structural engineering discipline, zero-pressure inspections, and long-term home security.

OUR GUIDING ETHOS

"We believe homeowners deserve clear explanations before they commit to repair work."

FoundationGuard was established to replace high-pressure sales tactics with honest diagnostic engineering. When a homeowner notices basement water or a wall crack, they don't need panic—they need accurate facts, structural measurements, and straightforward options.

Our Promise:

We will never recommend unnecessary structural piering when localized crack sealing or yard drainage will solve the problem.

Our Core Operating Values

1. Diagnostic Precision

We measure soil hydro-static pressure, wall deflection, and concrete strength before writing any quote.

2. Certified Field Crew

Our technicians undergo continuous safety training and manufacturer product certifications.

3. Respect for Your Home

Dust curtains, HEPA air filtration, and floor runners are installed prior to any concrete cutting.
